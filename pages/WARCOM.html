04 / WEARING CONFIGURATIONS

WEARING
CONFIGURATIONS

The WARCOM12™ is supplied with hardware for three wearing configurations. Conversion requires no tools and does not disturb electronic components, allowing reconfiguration in the field as helmets, roles, or operators change.

Helmet mounted, the WARCOM12™ attaches directly to standard side accessory rails and is compatible with PASGT, MICH, ACH, FAST (high cut), mid cut, Mark 7, Ops-Core™ and other ballistic helmet systems. The sliding arms are independently adjustable and hold the ear cups in position during vigorous activity, while allowing them to be lifted clear of the ear without removing the helmet.

The same headset reverts to the over-the-head configuration in seconds when a helmet is not worn, using the supplied headband.
