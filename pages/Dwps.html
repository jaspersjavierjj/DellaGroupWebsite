TIER 2

The AQUAPURE Pulse Resonance Smart Water Purifier is an advanced water treatment system utilizing pulse resonance purification technology to deliver clean drinking water without traditional filter consumables. Designed for large-scale deployment, it combines intelligent monitoring, low maintenance requirements, and continuous water quality management.

03 / KEY FEATURES

Intelligent purification without conventional filter consumables.

Pulse resonance technology combines physical water treatment, continuous monitoring, simplified maintenance, and scalable production for large deployment environments.

01

PURE PHYSICAL WATER PURIFICATION

Treats water through a physical pulse-resonance process without relying on chemical additives or conventional filtration media.

02

NO FILTER REPLACEMENT REQUIRED

Eliminates routine filter replacement to reduce maintenance requirements, consumable costs, and operational downtime.

03

INTELLIGENT CLOUD MONITORING

Supports connected monitoring of water quality and consumption data for more efficient system management.

04

LARGE-SCALE WATER PRODUCTION

Designed to provide continuous purified-water output for communities, field facilities, and other high-demand applications.

04 / PURIFICATION TECHNOLOGY

PULSE RESONANCE WATER PURIFICATION

Advantages of the pulse water purification system:

  1. Pure physical water purification can improve residents' drinking-water hygiene safely and on a large scale.
  2. Supply water by quality, improve the utilization rate of water resources for residents, and save water.
  3. It can effectively reduce white pollution such as barreled and bottled water, while promoting environmental awareness.
  4. Save labor and avoid risks associated with transportation and storage of bottled water.
  5. Provide strategic reserve water for emergencies, with the storage tank supplying additional clean drinking water.
  6. Low investment and convenient use allow residents to access high-quality and affordable purified water.
  7. Uses modern Internet of Things technology to build a real-time, intelligent, and controllable drinking-water management platform.

05 / SYSTEM STRUCTURE

WATER PURIFIER STRUCTURE

  1. Water outlet pipe Purified water output.
  2. Return pipe Returns circulating water to keep it fresh.
  3. Sewage pipe Discharges separated heavy metals and impurities as concentrated sewage, which may be used as domestic water for greening and similar applications.
  4. Power supply 220V AC.
  5. Pulse motor Silent permanent-magnet motor.
  6. Water pump Lifts water up to 36 meters, approximately 10 floors.
  7. Water storage tank Capacity of 0.3 tons.
  8. Cloud data monitoring system Monitors water quality and consumption.
  9. Adjustable angle bracket Stainless-steel, waterproof and moisture-proof construction with a 30 mm adjustment range.
  10. Pulse resonance water purification core machine Uses pulse resonance blasting for impurity separation.
