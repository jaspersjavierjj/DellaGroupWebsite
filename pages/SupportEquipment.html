ELECTRO-HYDRAULIC RESCUE TOOL

The Electro-Hydraulic Rescue Tool (EHRT) is a battery-powered, all- electric rescue device designed for rapid emergency response operations. Equipped with detachable cone claws and a high-performance electro- hydraulic system, it delivers powerful cutting, spreading, and prying capabilities while maintaining one-handed operation and portability. Its integrated control panel, lithium battery power source, and rugged construction make it ideal for vehicle extrication, disaster response, and technical rescue applications.

The Foldable Entrenching Shovel is designed for military, emergency response, and outdoor field operations requiring compact and reliable digging tools.
