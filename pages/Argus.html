ARGUS AISR-2

AI AUTONOMOUS ISR DRONE

TIER 3

An advanced surveillance platform integrating AI-assisted vision, automated target detection, multi-sensor monitoring, and persistent reconnaissance capabilities. Argus enhances command-level situational awareness by supporting automated perimeter surveillance and intelligent reconnaissance while maintaining human decision authority in operational employment.

03 / KEY FEATURES

TACTICAL FLIGHT FEATURES

Argus combines low-signature propulsion, extended-range communications, thermal imaging integration, and a rigid tactical airframe to support persistent autonomous reconnaissance and intelligent surveillance missions.

01

LOW-NOISE PROPELLERS

The 1050 3-blade design is engineered to provide efficient thrust, stable flight performance, and reduced acoustic signature, enabling discreet surveillance operations.

02

EXTENDED RANGE ANTENNA

The 5.8G SMA L150mm antenna provides stable signal transmission for long-distance control and real-time video telemetry during reconnaissance operations.

03

FPV THERMAL CAMERA MOUNT

Front-mounted IRC-640CA module enabling real-time aerial observation and situational awareness for tactical monitoring.

04

HEAVY-DUTY FRAME STRUCTURE

Lightweight yet rigid structure designed for high durability, vibration resistance, and stable flight performance in demanding operational environments.
