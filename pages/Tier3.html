ORION PRS-1
[Personal Reconnaissance System Drone]

ORION PRS-1

A compact, holster-carried reconnaissance drone designed for individual soldiers, law enforcement personnel, and special operations teams. Orion provides immediate aerial situational awareness without requiring a dedicated drone operator or extensive setup. The system is envisioned to support route reconnaissance, urban surveillance, building and compound inspection, ambush detection, force protection, border security, maritime boarding operations, and search and rescue support.

SENTINEL ISR-2
[TACTICAL ISR DRONE]

SENTINEL ISR-2

A lightweight tactical surveillance drone designed to provide real-time aerial monitoring and situational awareness for squad and platoon-level operations. Sentinel supports reconnaissance, patrol overwatch, perimeter security, and tactical surveillance across urban, jungle, coastal, and maritime environments through rapid deployment and persistent observation capabilities.

ARGUS AISR-2

An advanced surveillance platform integrating AI-assisted vision, automated target detection, multi-sensor monitoring, and persistent reconnaissance capabilities. Argus enhances command-level situational awareness by supporting automated perimeter surveillance and intelligent reconnaissance while maintaining human decision authority in operational employment.

ATLAS LOG-1
[Autonomous Logistics Support Drone]

ATLAS LOG-1

A heavy-lift unmanned logistics platform being developed to provide autonomous resupply capability for dispersed and forward-deployed forces. Atlas is intended to transport mission-essential supplies, medical equipment, ammunition, and sustainment packages while reducing personnel exposure during logistical operations.
