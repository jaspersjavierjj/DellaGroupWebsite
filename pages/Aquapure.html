WATER PURIFICATION

A filter-free purification platform designed for continuous, intelligent, and large-scale drinking-water production.

KEY FEATURES

Clean water through physical separation, intelligent monitoring, and low-maintenance operation.

01

PURE PHYSICAL WATER PURIFICATION

Uses pulse resonance blasting and separation to purify water without chemical additives.

02

NO FILTER REPLACEMENT REQUIRED

Eliminates traditional filter consumables, reducing maintenance, replacement work, and operating costs.

03

INTELLIGENT CLOUD MONITORING

Monitors water quality and consumption through a connected cloud-based management platform.

04

LARGE-SCALE WATER PRODUCTION

Produces up to 7.2 tons of purified water per day for high-demand and community-scale applications.
