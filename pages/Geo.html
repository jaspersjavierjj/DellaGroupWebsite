GEO FEATURE SUMMARY &PRODUCT OVERVIEW

GEO STREET LIGHT / SATURN 150W

1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW

GEO is an intelligent street lighting system designed for energy efficiency, retrofit compatibility, and simple plug-and-play deployment. It supports smart dimming, direct Wi-Fi control without apps or internet, and synchronized scheduling for reliable day-to-day operation.

2. FEATURE SUMMARY

•

Dynamic Light Control — High power lighting during peak traffic; low power lighting during low traffic; supports approximately 30–45% energy savings.

•

Easy-to-Use Technology — No app, no internet, and no data connection needed; controlled with a simple Wi-Fi connection.

•

GPS Time & Date Synchronization — Uses GPS time and location for accurate scheduling.

•

Adjustable Time Schedules — 12 adjustable time schedules for customized on, off, and dimming functions.

•

Magic Click Tool — Resets connected lamps in one street with a simple three-toggle on/off sequence.
