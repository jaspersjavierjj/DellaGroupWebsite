ATLAS LOG-1

AUTONOMOUS LOGISTICS SUPPORT DRONE

TIER 3

A heavy-lift unmanned logistics platform being developed to provide autonomous resupply capability for dispersed and forward-deployed forces. Atlas is intended to transport mission-essential supplies, medical equipment, ammunition, and sustainment packages while reducing personnel exposure during logistical operations.

03 / KEY FEATURES

HEAVY-LIFT FLIGHT PLATFORM FEATURES

Atlas is structured as a heavy-lift logistics UAV platform combining high-power propulsion, rigid structural reinforcement, and payload support stability for autonomous supply transport in operational environments.

01

HEAVY-LIFT PROPULSION SYSTEM

High-power brushless motors paired with optimized propellers to generate stable thrust and efficient lifting capability for logistics payload operations.

02

REINFORCED CENTRAL AIRFRAME

Structural drone body engineered to house flight electronics and maintain rigidity for stable heavy-lift operations.

03

PAYLOAD SUPPORT STABILIZATION

Heavy-duty support structure engineered to maintain drone stability and protect cargo during payload operations.
