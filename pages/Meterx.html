MeterX

AGE OF INNOVATION

DIGITAL DATA

The MeterX device, a retrofit attachment for existing meters, is programmable on a monthly basis to capture an image, convert it to digital data in real time, and seamlessly transmit it to a secure cloud server for an automated billing system, thereby enhancing efficiency and accuracy.

02 / SYSTEM CAPABILITIES

METERX RETROFIT
WIRELESS CONNECTED

Simplest way to manage electricity and power consumption through direct and automated data transfer.

CONNECTED UTILITY INTELLIGENCE

MeterX adds a programmable digital intelligence layer to existing analog or digital electricity meters without replacing the installed meter.

GSM LoRa 5G NB-IoT Wi-Fi Cloud
01

Direct Meter-to-Provider Transfer

Transfers electricity-meter data directly to the service provider, reducing manual collection, transportation, and encoding requirements.

02

GSM Module Connectivity

Uses a GSM module to transmit captured meter readings and digital records securely to the cloud for automated billing and monitoring.

03

LoRa and 5G NB-IoT Ready

Designed to support future LoRa and direct 5G NB-IoT connectivity for broader coverage, including remote and hard-to-reach locations.

04

Unlimited Device Scalability

Supports an unlimited number of connected MeterX devices, giving utilities and industries broader access across multiple sites.

05

Wi-Fi Connection

Can also connect through Wi-Fi for local configuration, data access, system management, and maintenance support.

06

Built-In Monitoring and Alerts

Records on fixed or remotely adjusted schedules, monitors meter temperature, reboots after system faults, and sends alerts for tampering, movement, weak batteries, and maintenance requirements.

01

Verified Digital Records

Picture references with date and time stamps reduce disputes and remove manual reading errors.

02

Automated Billing Workflow

Monthly schedules and cloud uploads improve billing speed, consistency, and operational efficiency.

03

Remote Service Visibility

Online location data, automated alerts, and remote access reduce delays in servicing distributed meters.
