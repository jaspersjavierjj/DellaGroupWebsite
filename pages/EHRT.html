ELECTRO-HYDRAULIC
RESCUE TOOL

EHRT-SE-01

Support Equipment

The Electro-Hydraulic Rescue Tool (EHRT) is a battery-powered, all-electric rescue device designed for rapid emergency response operations. Equipped with detachable cone claws and a high-performance electro-hydraulic system, it delivers powerful cutting, spreading, and prying capabilities while maintaining one-handed operation and portability. Its integrated control panel, lithium battery power source, and rugged construction make it ideal for vehicle extrication, disaster response, and technical rescue applications.

03 / KEY FEATURES

RAPID RESCUE CAPABILITY

Battery-powered hydraulic performance, quick-change tooling, simplified one-handed control, and centralized system monitoring support fast emergency response in demanding rescue environments.

01

CONE CLAW

Detachable conical claws allow quick replacement for tearing, spreading, squeezing, and more.

02

INTEGRATED ELECTRO-HYDRAULIC POWER SYSTEM

Efficient lithium battery with a built-in electro-hydraulic system delivers stable power without requiring an external pump.

03

ONE-HANDED OPERATION & BATTERY STATUS

Star-shaped handle valve supports simple one-handed use while a three-level battery indicator provides quick power-status visibility.

04

CENTRALIZED OPERATOR CONTROL PANEL

Integrates the power switch, battery and load indicators, work light, and direction control into a single operator interface.
