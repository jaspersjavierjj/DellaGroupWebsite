ORION PRS-1

PERSONAL RECONNAISSANCE SYSTEM DRONE

TIER 3

A compact, holster-carried reconnaissance drone designed for individual soldiers, law enforcement personnel, and special operations teams. Orion provides immediate aerial situational awareness without requiring a dedicated drone operator or extensive setup. The system is envisioned to support route reconnaissance, urban surveillance, building and compound inspection, ambush detection, force protection, border security, maritime boarding operations, and search and rescue support.

03 / KEY FEATURES

PERSONAL RECONNAISSANCE SYSTEM FEATURES

Orion is built for immediate field use, combining portability, rapid deployment, and lightweight mission-ready construction to extend real-time situational awareness to the individual operator.

01

Foldable Tactical Surveillance Drone

A compact and deployable unmanned aerial system designed for rapid field operations, providing real-time aerial surveillance, reconnaissance, and situational awareness. Its foldable design enables easy transport, quick deployment, and reliable performance in challenging environments.

02

QUICK THROW-LAUNCH MECHANISM

Immediate drone release and automatic stabilization after launch, allowing operators to deploy the UAV quickly in time-sensitive missions.

03

FOLDABLE & Lightweight Construction

Lightweight, rigid structure designed for durability, vibration resistance, and stable flight in demanding environments.

04 / OPERATOR VISION SYSTEM

Dual-Mode Tactical Smart Goggles

A lightweight tactical vision system designed for modern field operations, featuring dual functionality that allows operators to switch between normal optical view and real-time drone camera view through dedicated toggle switches located on the right side of the goggles.

05 / SMART GOGGLES FEATURES

TACTICAL GOGGLE SYSTEM FEATURES

Integrated viewing functions provide the operator with seamless transition between direct observation and drone-fed perspective during reconnaissance operations.

Vision Mode Selector

A side-mounted control switch enables quick switching between normal optical view and drone camera view, enhancing situational awareness.

Tactical Protective Housing

Durable, lightweight housing protects internal electronics and offers a compact, ergonomic design for field operations.

Real-Time Visual Interface

Front-facing camera module for live imagery and remote drone perspective viewing in reconnaissance missions.

06 / CARRY SYSTEM

Integrated Wearable Drone Holster System

The lightweight tactical holster allows hands-free movement while keeping the drone protected, accessible, and ready for immediate launch when required.

07 / HOLSTER FEATURES
